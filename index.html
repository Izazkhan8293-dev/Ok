About New Royal Electricals

Over five decades of trust, quality service, and community commitment in Pennadam.

Founded in 1970, New Royal Electricals & Hardware has grown to become Pennadam's most trusted destination for electrical, hardware, and building material supplies. For more than 55 years, we have served homeowners, electricians, contractors, builders, and industrial clients across the region — delivering genuine products at fair prices.

Our store carries an extensive range of products from leading national brands. Whether you are wiring a new home, upgrading your lighting, laying pipelines, or painting your walls, we have everything you need under one roof — with knowledgeable staff ready to guide you.

What We Offer

  • Electrical switches, sockets & modular accessories (GM, Kundan, Hi-Fi, Fybros)
  • LED bulbs, tube lights, panel lights & lighting accessories (Crompton, Philips, Norwood)
  • Motor pumps & submersible sets (CRI, Laxmi Motors)
  • PVC, UPVC, CPVC pipes & GI fitting items
  • Birla Opus Paints — authorised dealer for interior & exterior finishes
  • Hardware tools, fasteners & building accessories

Goods once sold cannot be taken back. This policy is printed on every invoice issued by our store.

New Royal Electricals operates under a strict no-return, no-refund policy for all sold goods. We urge all customers to inspect products carefully at the time of purchase and raise any concerns with our staff before leaving the store.

Limited Exceptions

  • Manufacturing defects identified at the time of purchase, verified by our staff, may be eligible for exchange.
  • Items delivered incorrectly due to our error will be replaced at no charge to the customer.
  • Defective items must be reported within 24 hours of purchase, accompanied by the original bill.

Non-Returnable Items

  • All electrical wires and cables — once cut or used
  • All paint products — once mixed or opened
  • Special-order or custom-sourced items
  • Items returned without original packaging or bill

For any queries regarding this policy, please contact us at 94431 82381 with your bill number ready.
